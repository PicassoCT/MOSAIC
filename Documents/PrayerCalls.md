# MOSAIC Enclave Prayer Calls

Production target: short territorial broadcasts, roughly 12–16 seconds each. They should sound credible over damaged civic loudspeakers and remain clearly distinguishable when heard at a distance. Each prompt deliberately uses one of three performance forms: sung congregation, rhythmic chant, or a prayer read by one preacher.

## Children of Elon

**Performance:** Sung prayer — massed congregation  
**Suggested asset:** `sounds/civilian/religions/children_of_elon/call_to_prayer_01.ogg`

**Prayer call**

> Raise your dishes, antennas awake. Father of Orbits, receive our ping and return our signal. Through storm, blackout and broken groundlink, let no packet be lost. All ground beneath the constellation is heard.

**Music prompt**

Territorial orbital-megachurch hymn sung by a large mixed congregation in strict unison and broad open fifths; no soloist; slow 64 BPM; battered brass, enormous marching bass drum, satellite telemetry chirps and shortwave dropouts; projected from rooftop horn arrays with long concrete-canyon echoes; proud, serene and technologically messianic; 15 seconds, decisive hard ending.

## Church of the Sim

**Performance:** Single-preacher prayer  
**Suggested asset:** `sounds/civilian/religions/church_of_the_sim/call_to_prayer_01.ogg`

**Prayer call**

> Citizens, assume the T-Pose. Face the nearest camera. Administrator beyond the skybox: allocate memory, preserve our state and grant us one more hour of uptime. This district remains rendered. This district will not be unloaded.

**Music prompt**

One exhausted but commanding televangelist preaching through an overpowered municipal PA; no choir and no sung phrases; cooling-fan drone tuned to D, a boot chime, one ceremonial snare roll and a final low-brass blast; clipped broadcast compression and slapback between towers; begins confidentially and swells into an enclave-wide declaration of continued existence; 14 seconds.

## Divine Order of Necessary Taboos

**Performance:** Chanted prayer — caller and congregation  
**Suggested asset:** `sounds/civilian/religions/divine_order_of_necessary_taboos/call_to_prayer_01.ogg`

**Prayer call**

> CALLER: Attend the warning!  
> CONGREGATION: We attend!  
> CALLER: If it is written?  
> CONGREGATION: It has happened!  
> CALLER: If it is forbidden?  
> CONGREGATION: Someone tried it!  
> ALL: All error negated. Only truth remains.

**Music prompt**

Antiphonal bureaucratic cult chant: one stern female caller answered by a disciplined mass congregation at 78 BPM; warning beeps establish the pitch, with stamping boots, steel clipboard strikes and a sustained emergency-siren pedal; loudspeaker distortion and severe rhythmic gating; every response grows louder and more territorial, like regulations imposed on every street within hearing; 16 seconds.

## Brotherhood of God Hackers

**Performance:** Single-preacher prayer  
**Suggested asset:** `sounds/civilian/religions/brotherhood_of_god_hackers/call_to_prayer_01.ogg`

**Prayer call**

> Reality has an interface. Every miracle leaves a port. Carry the Sign. Submit the prayer. Escalate the exploit. No firewall encloses us. No skybox contains us. Administrator beyond the world: answer—or be patched.

**Music prompt**

A single pirate-radio preacher with a fast, controlled liturgical cadence; absolutely no choir or singing; sub-bass carrier hum, checksum beeps, modem shrieks cut into ritual punctuation and one distant air-raid horn; overdriven voice routed through a hijacked city PA with long asymmetric delay; insurgent certainty and technological blasphemy announcing control of the spectrum; 14 seconds.

## Big Andian Buddhist Church of the Avatara

**Performance:** Sung prayer — processional choir  
**Suggested asset:** `sounds/civilian/religions/big_andian_avatara/call_to_prayer_01.ogg`

**Prayer call**

> Be still beneath the mountain. Attend the child, the stranger and the impossible thought. The Outside speaks through living vessels. May the Visitor wake gently. The mountain speaks; the valley listens.

**Music prompt**

Slow high-altitude processional prayer sung by layered low female and male voices in parallel fifths, with breathy overtone textures; no spoken preacher; conch-like mountain horn, a huge skin drum every four beats, wind and natural stone echo; begins as a remote ridge signal and expands until it occupies the entire valley; solemn, patient and unmistakably sovereign; 16 seconds.

## Headdrummer Cult

**Performance:** Chanted prayer — synchronized collective  
**Suggested asset:** `sounds/civilian/religions/headdrummer_cult/call_to_prayer_01.ogg`

**Prayer call**

> One wall. One beat. One thought beneath the implant. Eat. Pray. Drum. Hear our walls and know our number. Beat against the noise until the hidden mind answers.

**Music prompt**

Compulsive unison chant locked to four hollow impacts at 92 BPM; no conventional melody and no instruments beyond bodies, concrete and one distorted implant tone; voices multiply on every cycle, with close breath and brutal courtyard echo; unsettling machine-perfect synchronization used as a territorial acoustic marker; 12 seconds, ending in abrupt total silence.
