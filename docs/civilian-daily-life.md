# Civilian daily life and readable conversations

The goal is plausible behaviour that players can learn and imitate. Civilian
routines should supply useful cover without turning every idle animation into
a test that only the ambient AI can pass.

## Controls

- Hover over a visible civilian for a brief moment to read their current or
  recent conversation. Quiet companions can overhear a nearby group exchange.
- Hover and press **O** to toggle continued observation. **Delete** clears the
  existing observation marks. The panel follows the most recent exchange among
  observed units; hovering takes precedence.
- Use **Guard** on a civilian to accompany them and wait with their group.
- The explicit **Blend in** command on disguised operatives accepts a civilian,
  building or ground position. It accompanies the civilian or approaches and
  waits at the destination. New player orders replace it. It requires cloak.

Hover and Observe only read speech already generated by the simulation. Moving
the mouse does not start a conversation, change random choices or grant vision.
Recent speech expires after 45 seconds; nearby overheard speech expires sooner.

## Routines

Ambient Gaia pedestrians remember a home, a regular destination and persistent
social, cautious and curious tendencies. They interleave routine trips with
errands, stop at passable building approaches and can seek nearby shelter in
rain. Existing engine pathfinding still handles travel between these points.

Some civilians form pairs or groups of up to four. Companions use Guard; leaders
occasionally wait for stragglers. Separated members eventually leave the group,
and a surviving member takes over when the leader disappears. Civilians and
groups being guarded by an operative are protected from population/stuck cleanup.

Visits generally take 12–28 seconds. Completed shopping/errand visits can add a
shopping bag; airport/hotel visits add a travel bag. Returning home clears bags.
The existing handbag piece represents travel luggage. Baby/trolley carriers do
not receive another incompatible carrying pose. Disguises use the same bag
functions after a completed accompanied visit or a deliberate visit at a venue.
Interrupted visits do not count as completed accompanied errands.

Some passers-by linger near a registered brothel for 25–65 seconds, occasionally
shifting position. Registration comes from the building's actual brothel
hologram, rather than a guessed location. The behaviour does not imply that all
civilians in the area are customers.

Eligible Gaia passenger cars and buses can pause at route waypoints. Very rare
pedestrians emerge beside a stopped vehicle during population replenishment, or
walk up to one and disappear. Vehicles must have been stationary for four seconds.
There is a citywide 20-second opportunity limit and a 150-second vehicle cooldown;
probability, proximity and eligibility further reduce the frequency. Boarding
cancels if the vehicle moves. Guard targets and grouped civilians do not board.
These are ambient population transitions, not simulated passenger manifests.

Nearby damage interrupts benign activities, causes varied short reactions and
escape/refuge movement, and leaves a temporary personal memory of the dangerous
area. Civilians avoid resuming an unsafe destination immediately. Aerosol states
retain their own control. Automatic routines never commandeer player operatives;
Guard/Blend in animate their visible disguise while preserving player orders.

## Conversations

Four-line exchanges replace randomly concatenated gossip. Characters can relay
recent errands, carrying luggage, leaving vehicles, violence, injury, lost homes
or companions, sheltering and lingering. Memory is bounded to six events and
twelve simulation minutes; recently retold events yield to other topics.
Slang slots vary complete sentences about rent, work, surveillance and city life.
They run locally and deterministically without an external generator service.

The conversation panel also receives existing intercepted operative exchanges.
Their sequence now starts at the first line, progresses at a readable rate and
uses the conversation UI instead of money-style floating text. Existing
operational and occasional conspiracy dialogue remains available.

## Integration and conflicts

- Automatic civilian decisions apply only to ambient Gaia pedestrians. The
  explicit cover command resolves the conflict between richer routines and
  preserving player command ownership.
- Ordinary danger can interrupt prayer/chat/phone activity. Aerosol behaviour
  remains authoritative; global anarchy/refugee logic remains in the main gadget.
- Venue names use public `city_street_name` data when present and a district
  description otherwise. The separate road/address work is not required or
  modified. Both tasks touch `scripts/lib_staticstring.lua`, so concurrent
  merges may require resolving that shared file. This change uses native
  pathfinding; it does not add a pedestrian road graph or indoor navigation.
- Command ID 34971 is reserved as `CMD_CIVILIAN_BLEND` in `commandsIDs.lua`.
- Behaviour frequencies, memory limits and timings live in
  `luarules/configs/civilian_daily_life.lua`.

## Review status and in-game checks

Source-reviewed against master `0857982c59c92f7e87e33d448232d6ab9bd25a0c`.
Automated Lua validation was intentionally not run under the user's existing
instruction. No Recoil runtime was available, so engine loading, animations,
pathfinding, multiplayer determinism and play balance still need an in-game check.

1. Start a normal populated game. Watch several trips, group waits, bag pickup
   and home drop-off. Check airports, hotel-labelled houses and brothel entrances.
2. Hover callers and chatting pairs, then press O and move the cursor away.
   Check wrapping, speaker order, stale-text expiry and no text through lost vision.
3. Guard a group with a cloaked operative; wait through a visit, then issue Move.
   Confirm the order takes effect, social poses end and the disguise follows.
   Repeat with Blend in on a civilian, building and ground point.
4. Cause local damage during a chat/prayer/visit. Check immediate interruption,
   sensible escape, delayed recovery and stories tied to the incident. Confirm
   an interrupted visit does not award a bag and aerosol control is preserved.
5. Watch stopped cars/buses over several minutes. Check rare boarding/exits,
   cancellation when a vehicle moves and no passenger emergence into obstacles.
6. Check group leader loss, population reduction, destroyed venues and command
   cancellation. Verify no stuck group or removed Guard target.
7. Run the scenario with two clients and a replay before merging. Compare
   behaviour and inspect engine logs for Lua errors and pathfinding cost.
