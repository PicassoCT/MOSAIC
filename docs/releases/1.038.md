# MOSAIC 1.038

- Investigation is an abstract construction with a hovering interrogation-hologram preview, exact unit targeting and 30 seconds of resource-funded build progress. Ordinary owned physical units can be investigated; the Repair cursor is removed.
- Car theft uses the buildable icon again. Capture preserves the original vehicle, selected model and payload. Ambient traffic and fleeing jobs stop issuing orders after capture.
- Trucks use driving profiles for their selected body and payload, including speed, acceleration and braking. Civilian traffic gains destination dwell and controlled intersection holds.
- The Police Scanner cycles through the latest three police events or enemy sightings.
- Sandstorms and smog make civilians mask up without removing masks worn for anarchy when the weather clears.
- Ground-walker gun and movement audio is quieter. Warning birds land smoothly. Helicopters gain rotor-blur presentation and improved ferry flight handling.
- The build menu hides blocked build options and refreshes when permissions change.

The game archive is installed directly in a Spring/Recoil `games` directory. Engine and map are separate downloads. Use only one archive for this version.

The Dhubay map changes are separately committed in `PicassoCT/MOSAIC_LastDayOfDubai` at `df10704`: west/east skyline placement, smoother coastline joins, southern desert extensions, green-fringe correction and ambient boat fixes.

Validation includes focused Lua 5.1 gameplay regressions, release-packaging tests and isolated engine archive/definition checks. These do not replace a rendered multiplayer gameplay test.
